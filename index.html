I'm Asongna Brilan Nso Nkengafac, a Hardware Developer based in Buea, Cameroon, with more than six years of experience turning ideas into working electronics. My work sits where robotics and product development meet: designing the circuit, laying out the PCB, writing the firmware, modelling the enclosure and putting intelligence at the edge.

I'm the Co-founder of PlastiBytes, a manufacturing and product development startup focused on advancing hardware development in Cameroon and across Africa. We help founders, researchers and companies go from prototype to product, built locally.

As part of the leadership team running the Hardware Innovation Valley Community (HWIVC), I help grow a network of engineers, makers and innovators who share tools, knowledge and opportunities. I believe Africa's next wave of technology will be built here, by people who understand the problems first-hand.
